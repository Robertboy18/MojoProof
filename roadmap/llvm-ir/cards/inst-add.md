# The `add` instruction

## Rule

LangRef's section on `add` (the quote is the first sentence of its overview). The importer reads `add` as `Inst.bin` with `BinOp.add` (`Parser.inst`); the semantics gives it a meaning in `MojoProof.Sem.exec`, with `MojoProof.Sem.intBin`. The block checker (MojoProof/Sym/Rules.lean): `Sym.exec_bin`.

## Corpus

111 of the 111 corpus files use it (9884 uses); `corpus` lists the first 5 in path order.

The programs of the library's `mojo_kernel` theorems use it (`tests/corpus/vadd/vadd_vector_addition_vector_addition6A6A_793e5fe36a195e73.nvptx.ll`, `tests/corpus/vmul/vector_multiply_vector_multiply_vector_multiply6A6A_227fc544da3deaff.nvptx.ll`, `tests/corpus/vsub/vector_subtraction_vector_subtraction_vector_subtr6A6A_a0b4d9d2b6e652e1.nvptx.ll`). L3 counts that only through a kernel card with a recorded GPU run, and no kernel has one.

## Out of scope

Forms of the instruction the corpus does not use are read by the same parser case, but no corpus file exercises them. That the semantics gives `add` the meaning LLVM gives it rests on reading LangRef (docs/TRUST.md T7), not on a check against LLVM.
