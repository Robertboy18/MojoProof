# The `sub` instruction

## Rule

LangRef's section on `sub` (the quote is the first sentence of its overview). The importer reads `sub` as `Inst.bin` with `BinOp.sub` (`Parser.inst`); the semantics gives it a meaning in `MojoProof.Sem.exec`, with `MojoProof.Sem.intBin`. MojoProofTest/Modelled.lean runs a kernel of it (its row `sub`), and the run returns. The block checker (MojoProof/Sym/Rules.lean): `Sym.exec_bin`.

75 of the 116 corpus files use it (1988 uses); `corpus` lists the first 5 in path order.

## Out of scope

Forms of the instruction the corpus does not use are read by the same parser case, but no corpus file exercises them. That the semantics gives `sub` the meaning LLVM gives it rests on reading LangRef (docs/TRUST.md T7), not on a check against LLVM.
